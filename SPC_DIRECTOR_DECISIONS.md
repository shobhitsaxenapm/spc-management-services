# SPC Management Services - Director's Decisions (May 2026)

Responses from the Director of SPC Management Services on key website decisions before prototype sharing.

---

## 1. Hiring Intelligence / Insights Section

**Question:** How critical is this section for SPC to highlight?

**Answer: B — Yes, but keep the presentation simple.**

---

## 2. Approach to AI Usage on the Website

**Question:** How should AI involvement be presented to the user?

**Answer: B — Keep it in the background and do not highlight it prominently.**

---

## 3. Type of Information for Insights

**Question:** What specific kind of market insights should we prioritize showing?

**Answer: D — A combination of all the above (hiring demand, hiring difficulty, salary and market benchmarks).**

---

## 4. "Predictive Talent Mapping" Section

**Question:** Do you want to keep a feature like this on the website?

**Answer: A — Yes, but ensure the content is realistic and simple.**

## 5. Call-to-Action (CTA) Button Strategy

**Question:** What primary action should a user take when clicking the CTA button?

**Answer: D — Not sure, please suggest a better action.**

---

## 6. Job Portal / Talent Hub Application Flow

**Question:** Which application process do you prefer?

**Answer: B — Application with resume upload plus basic screening questions.**

---

## 7. Overall Website Direction and Aesthetic

**Question:** What overall tone or 'feel' should the website convey?

**Answer: B — Insight-driven, emphasizing expertise and authority.**

---

## Summary of Key Takeaways

- **Insights section:** Include but keep it simple, not flashy.
- **AI:** Use it but keep it in the background — don't make it the headline.
- **Insights data:** Show hiring demand, hiring difficulty, and salary benchmarks — all combined.
- **Predictive Talent Mapping:** Keep it, but realistic and simple language only.
- **CTA:** Needs a better suggestion from the team — director is open to ideas.
- **Job applications:** Resume upload + basic screening questions (current implementation aligns).
- **Website tone:** Insight-driven, expertise and authority — not flashy tech, not plain traditional.
