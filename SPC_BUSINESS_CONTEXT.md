# SPC Management Services - Business Context & Website Guardrails

## Company Overview

SPC Management Services is an ISO 9001:2015 certified management consulting company established in 2004.

SPC operates as:

* A specialist recruitment partner
* A management consulting company
* A business process outsourcing (BPO) partner
* A project management support organization
* An HR and CSR advisory firm

SPC primarily serves Government organizations, Development Sector organizations, NGOs, INGOs, UN-supported programs, Public Health initiatives, and Corporate organizations across India.

# What SPC Is NOT

SPC is NOT:

* A SaaS company
* A recruiting marketplace
* A job board company
* An HR software platform
* An AI startup
* A workforce analytics software provider

The website should never position SPC as a software company.

---

# Website Positioning Principles

The website should position SPC as:

* Trusted
* Professional
* Execution-focused
* Experienced
* Ethical
* Insight-driven
* Domain expert
* Government and development sector specialist

The website should NOT feel like:

* A startup landing page
* A SaaS product website
* An AI-first product company
* A flashy technology company

---

# AI Usage Guidelines

AI should be used only where it creates practical business value.

Good examples:

* Hiring insights
* Talent planning assistance
* Workforce trend summaries
* Job matching support
* Recruitment efficiency tools

Avoid:

* Fake AI claims
* "Proprietary AI model" language unless proven
* Unrealistic predictions
* Complex AI terminology
* AI for the sake of AI

AI should be presented as a supporting capability, not the company's identity.

# Guardrails For Future Website Decisions

Whenever evaluating a new section, feature, AI tool, page, or design concept, ask:

1. Does this strengthen SPC's credibility?
2. Does this help clients trust SPC more?
3. Does this align with consulting, recruitment, outsourcing, or project support?
4. Would a government, NGO, or corporate stakeholder find this valuable?
5. Does this make SPC look like a service and consulting company rather than a software company?
6. Is this realistic and implementable?
7. Does this support lead generation or business development?

If the answer is "No" to most of these questions, reconsider the feature.
